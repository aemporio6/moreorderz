### Design note D-1 (owner / developer to confirm)
The spec (R-H-1, D-W1) keeps two layers: physical bin stock and lot ownership, with the lot chosen at Month Confirm. This build keeps **one** layer (lot × location): the system (never the picker) takes the oldest lot by the shop's owner rule when units leave the shelf, and Month Confirm books the cost from those units. Cost, owner and all accounting still happen only at Confirm. It differs from the spec only if lots are re-ordered between shipping and Confirm; it removes the separate reconciliation between two stock tables.
